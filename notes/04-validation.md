The final test. Do our scores actually pick out real disease mutations?

We compare against MITOMAP, a curated catalogue of mitochondrial variants,
which separates known disease-causing mutations from harmless ones found in
healthy people.

The benchmark to beat is ViennaRNA, the standard physics-based tool. If
Minerva's scores do not separate sick from healthy better than physics does,
the honest answer is to use physics.
